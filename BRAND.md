# NutriChef AI brand guide

## Name and taglines

**Primary tagline:** Cook what you have. Eat what you need.

Alternates for ads, social posts and app-store copy:
- Your fridge already has dinner in it.
- Recipes that start with your fridge.
- Less waste. Better plates. Zero guesswork.
- A cook who starts with your fridge.
- Dinner, sorted.

**Voice:** warm, plain-spoken and a little playful. Speak like a friend who cooks well and
knows some nutrition, not like a diet app. Avoid guilt, "clean eating" talk and medical claims.

## Logo

The mark is a plate (ring), a leaf (fresh food) and a saffron spark (the AI).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `public/brand/nutrichef-mark.svg` | On light backgrounds |
| `public/brand/nutrichef-mark-dark.svg` | On dark backgrounds |
| `public/favicon.svg` | Browser tab and app icon (filled beet circle) |

The wordmark is "NutriChef" in Instrument Serif with an italic beet-colored "ai".
Leave clear space around the mark equal to the height of the spark.

## Colors

| Name | Light mode | Dark mode | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rice paper | `#F2F3EC` | `#16121A` | Page background |
| Peppercorn | `#1C1A17` | `#F0EBE4` | Text |
| Beetroot | `#8C1D47` | `#E5729A` | Brand color, buttons, headline |
| Herb | `#2E6B4A` | `#86C9A1` | Handwritten notes, success |
| Saffron | `#E9A825` | `#F3C04F` | Small highlights, focus rings, the AI spark |

Beetroot is the hero color. Use saffron sparingly; it's the spark, not a fill.

## Type

- **Instrument Serif:** headlines and the wordmark. Large, tight, editorial.
- **Schibsted Grotesk:** body text, buttons and UI.
- **Reenie Beanie:** handwritten margin notes only, like pencil marks in a cookbook.
  Never use it for anything a person must read to use the site.

All three are free on Google Fonts.
